Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining, hosting, and security on GoogleMeet.

Do participants need an account to join?

No, participants can join directly by entering the room code shared by the host.

Is GoogleMeet free to use?

Yes, GoogleMeet provides free HD video conferencing and interactive virtual classrooms.

How many participants can join a meeting room?

GoogleMeet supports seamless multi-peer connections designed for classrooms, group discussions, and team syncs.

Are my calls and file transfers secure?

Yes, media feeds and file transfers use DTLS/SRTP end-to-end peer encryption with WebRTC technology.

Can I share my screen and write on a whiteboard simultaneously?

Yes, you can toggle screen sharing and open the collaborative canvas drawing board at any point during your call.

Open Whiteboard Studio during a call. Your board interactions automatically compile into video recordings visible under your host profile history.
